Design note

Keeping degree and confidence as two separate bars (rather than a bar + gauge, as in the real app) makes both metrics visually equal in weight and easier to scan side by side. This pairing is a good stress-test for the layout: Sunitinib has the maximum degree of interaction (5/5) alongside comparatively low confidence (38%) — with separate bars that contrast is immediately visible, which is exactly the case where Wireframe 1's fused single-number approach would be most likely to mislead.
