Effectiveness Summary

Curcumin is classified as Low Risk when paired with Sunitinib (an oral VEGFR/multi-kinase inhibitor). The mock degree-of-interaction score is 5 out of 5 — the maximum on this scale — meaning curcumin is predicted to have the most significant possible effect on this drug's pharmacokinetics, despite the low overall risk classification. Confidence in this specific estimate is comparatively low (38%): the source figure classifies the risk directly, but doesn't detail the underlying PK mechanism, so a high predicted interaction degree is paired here with real uncertainty about how firmly established that estimate is.

Design note

This screen is reached by tapping a search result on Quick Check, so it's a pushed detail view rather than its own tab — hence the back button instead of the bottom tab bar. Comparative Studies links out to supporting literature/context rather than comparing this pairing against other drugs numerically; the first two entries are illustrative placeholders (no real URL behind them), included to show the intended shape of the section, not to claim real citations exist yet.
