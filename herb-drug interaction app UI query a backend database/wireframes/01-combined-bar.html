Design note

The stoplight carries the actual risk classification (green/Low Risk) on its own, so the combined bar below it only has to represent degree-of-interaction + confidence — it doesn't need to imply anything about risk itself. That said, the marker position (69%) is still an average of two independent numbers (5/5 and 38%) — a viewer can't tell from the bar alone that this pairing actually has the maximum predicted interaction degree paired with comparatively low confidence. The raw numbers below the bar exist to compensate for that ambiguity.
